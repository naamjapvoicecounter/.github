## 🌸 Our Mission & Vision

At **Naam Jap Voice Counter**, we believe that daily spiritual remembrance (*Naam Smaran & Japa Sadhana*) should be completely effortless, accessible, and unburdened by commercial distractions:

- **No Enforced Limits**: Sadhana is deeply personal. We empower devotees to chant according to their inner calling — 11, 21, 54, 108, 1008, or continuous non-stop remembrance.
- **Pure Privacy First**: 100% client-side architecture. Spiritual data and counters are private and remain securely on the devotee's device.
- **Heritage Meets Modern Innovation**: Real-time browser speech AI, Bluetooth car navigation integration, haptic stealth tapping, and authentic Vedic mantra repositories.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Capabilities

- 🎙️ **Smart Voice Counter**: AI Speech recognition that counts chants in real time without touching the screen.
- 📿 **Touch & Auto Counter**: Tactile bead with haptic vibration feedback and customizable interval speed metronome.
- 🤫 **Secret Mode (Gupt Sadhana)**: Discreet stealth interface designed for private chanting in office meetings, metros, and public spaces.
- 🚗 **Car Mode**: Spoken Bluetooth and speaker audio announcements for hands-free counting while driving.
- 🧘 **Dhyan Mode (Zen Meditation)**: Minimalist screen with rhythmic breathing aura and hardware keyboard controls.
- 🌸 **Dynamic Mantra Themes**: Automatic aesthetic transformations based on your chosen deity (Vrindavan, Ayodhya, Kailash, Shakti, etc.).
- 💾 **Persistent Local Storage**: Counts auto-save locally in real time — never wipes out or empties unexpectedly.
- 📥 **One-Click Backup & Restore**: Full encrypted JSON and CSV export/import for effortless device migration.
- 📜 **50+ Authentic Vedic Mantras**: Sanskrit slokas, comprehensive spiritual meanings, and audio pronunciation.
- 📲 **Offline PWA App**: Installable native web app with zero app store bloat and offline functionality.
