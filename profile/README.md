## 🌟 Why Naam Jap Voice Counter?

Traditional counting often forces devotees to compromise — either by carrying a physical mala in inconvenient environments (while driving, cooking, walking, or at work) or by tapping a distracting screen filled with popups. 

**Naam Jap Voice Counter** bridges this gap with a modern, high-tech, reverence-first design:

- **🎙️ Hands-Free Voice AI**: Built-in speech recognition listens to your chanting in real time and increments your counter automatically.
- **🤫 Secret Mode (Gupt Sadhana)**: A completely blacked-out, stealth AMOLED interface designed for private chanting during office hours, in the metro, or in public.
- **🚗 Automotive Car Mode**: Spoken audio announcements over Bluetooth / car speakers (at 11, 21, 54, 108 milestones) so you stay safe on the road.
- **🧘 Dhyan Mode (Zen Meditation)**: Minimalist screen with a rhythmic breathing aura and hardware keyboard navigation (Spacebar / Arrow keys).
- **🌸 Adaptive Mantra Themes**: Visual ambiences that automatically adapt to your chosen deity (Vrindavan, Ayodhya, Kashi, Kailash, etc.).
- **💾 Never-Empty Local Storage**: Your counts are saved instantly to your browser's local memory — your progress is permanent and will never be cleared accidentally.
- **📥 Encrypted Backup & Restore**: One-click export and import of your entire spiritual history in JSON or CSV.
- **📜 50+ Authentic Vedic Repositories**: Sanskrit mantras with accurate English transliteration, spiritual significance, and audio pronunciation.
